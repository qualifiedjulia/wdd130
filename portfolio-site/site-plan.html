Overview

Purpose

The purpose of my site is to have a place where I can put the best of my art that I want to show off to people. I want to use this website as a place where people can answer the questiond "What is Julia up to?" and "What kind of art does Julia make?".

Audience

The main audience for this site is future employers to show them what I am capable of. However this is also a great place to send friends and family members when they want to know what I am up to and what I have done.

Home page

Welcome

Making art is the thing in my life that I feel most passionate about. Part of being an artist means showing my work to other people, so I have made this website to do just that. Whether you are a friend, family member, employer, or just someone who wants to see my art, I can't wait for you to see all the different things I've made and worked hard on.

About Me

My name is Julia Garcia. I am currently in my third year of studying illustration at BYUI. By day I'm doing homework and making art. By night I'm playing video games, crocheting, and watching cartoons. When do I sleep? Never.

My dream after I graduate is to make comic books, however I love to make many different types of art, and I hope I can try all different types of art. I've tried many different mediums such as painting, sewing, a little bit of animating, among other things as well.

About My Art

I love using many different mediums and having different focuses in my art, but my favorite illustrations have to be my digital works. I would say that they are the illustrations I am able to personally connect with.

I also love making character driven illustrations. Pieces of art that focus on the personality and motivations of one or more characters is my bread and butter. This falls hand in hand with my love and desire to make comic books as well as make illustrations for children's books and novels. Different forms of media that focus on specific characters are the core of where I desire to take my art as I improve and especially after I graduate.

I also plan on designing my website so that when you click on an illustration, you'll be able to see a bigger version of that image along with a text box that will have more information about the artwork (such as the medium that was used, size of the original artwork, what I made the artwork for, and an artist's statement in some cases)

Other Works

This section is for all the other projects I have worked on and am currently working on. This includes stories I have written, comics I have made, and videos I have animated.
